Architecture & Pedagogy

Teaching Notes & System Specifications

Learner profiling, unconstrained audio-first instructional design, and Kokoro TTS speech synthesis specifications.

Learner Profile & Tone

  • Audience: Everyday Indian citizens, tenants, employees, consumers, and digital banking users with no formal legal background.
  • Pedagogical Stance: Empathetic, empowering, objective, and practical. Jargon is introduced only alongside immediate plain-English expansions.
  • Active Retrieval Priority: Fluency during listening is reinforced through mandatory checkpoint pauses and 3D flashcards practice.

Audio & Kokoro TTS Specifications

Parameter Configuration Rationale
TTS Engine Kokoro TTS (`/Users/vault/.local/bin/kokoro-tts-tool`) Local, offline-ready 82M model with natural cadence and no cloud API costs.
Narrator Voice am_adam (Grade A- Narrator) Deep, authoritative, highly intelligible voice suited for legal exposition.
Pronunciation Voice am_adam @ 0.95x speed Slightly deliberate tempo to ensure clean phonetics on Latin maxims and doctrines.
Audio Packaging 24,000 Hz WAV concatenated into 128kbps MP3 Lightweight, fast browser streaming with sub-10MB footprints per 7-minute lesson.
