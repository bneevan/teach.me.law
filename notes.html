Teaching Notes & System Specifications

Learner profiling, unconstrained audio-first instructional design, and Kokoro TTS speech synthesis specifications.

Learner Profile & Tone

Audience Everyday Indian citizens, tenants, employees, consumers, and digital banking users with no formal legal background.
Pedagogical Stance Empathetic, empowering, objective, and practical. Technical legalese is introduced only alongside immediate plain-English expansions.
Active Retrieval Priority Fluency during listening is continuously tested and reinforced through mandatory checkpoint pauses and 3D flashcard retrieval practice.

Audio & Kokoro TTS Specifications

Parameter Configuration Rationale
TTS Engine Kokoro TTS (`/Users/vault/.local/bin/kokoro-tts-tool`) Local, offline-ready 82M neural model with natural cadence and zero cloud API latency or dependencies.
Narrator Voice am_adam (American Male Adam) Deep, authoritative, highly intelligible voice suited for legal exposition and civic rights instruction.
Pronunciation Voice am_adam @ 0.95× speed Slightly deliberate tempo to ensure clean phonetics on Latin maxims and constitutional doctrines.
Audio Packaging 24,000 Hz WAV concatenated into 128kbps MP3 Lightweight, fast browser streaming with sub-8MB footprints per 7-minute lesson.
Synchronisation Segment Timelines JSON Deterministic cue points for seamless visual card flipping, progress bars, and checkpoint triggers.
