Verified Indian Legal Portals & Databases

Curated high-trust official government portals, Supreme Court databases, cyber helpline channels, and verified community legal desks across India.

Official Statutory Law & Judicial Databases

India Code (Ministry of Law & Justice)

Official digital repository of all Central & State Bare Acts (BNS, BNSS, Indian Contract Act 1872, CPA 2019, IT Act 2000, DPDP Act 2023).

indiacode.nic.in ↗

Indian Kanoon

Search engine for all Indian Supreme Court, High Court, and Tribunal judgments (*D.K. Basu*, *Lalita Kumari*, *Jacob Mathew*, *Puttaswamy*).

indiankanoon.org ↗

eCourts Services Portal & App

Case status, cause lists, daily orders, and certified copy applications across 3,000+ District and Subordinate Court complexes in India.

ecourts.gov.in ↗

e-Daakhil Consumer Portal

Ministry of Consumer Affairs portal to file online claims before District, State, and National Consumer Commissions without an advocate.

edaakhil.nic.in ↗

NALSA & Free Legal Aid

Statutory authority providing free legal aid advocates to women, children, undertrials, and low-income citizens under Article 39A.

nalsa.gov.in ↗

Cybercrime, Banking & Digital Rights Helplines

National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal & 1930

Ministry of Home Affairs portal for financial fraud reporting, Golden Hour inter-bank freezes, and cyber extortion FIR registration.

cybercrime.gov.in ↗

RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (CMS)

Statutory escalation portal for unreversed unauthorized bank transactions, failed ATM withdrawals, and customer protection circular defaults.

cms.rbi.org.in ↗

DoT Sanchar Saathi & Chakshu Portal

Department of Telecommunications portal to verify SIM connections in your name, block stolen phones (CEIR), and report fraudulent calls.

sancharsaathi.gov.in ↗

CERT-In (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team)

National nodal agency for reporting corporate data leaks, vulnerabilities, and unauthorized access to computer systems.

cert-in.org.in ↗

Public Accountability & Practical Field Desks

RTI Online Portal

Official portal to file Right to Information applications and first appeals to Central government ministries and public authorities.

rtionline.gov.in ↗

National Human Rights Commission (NHRC)

Statutory body investigating custodial torture, police excesses, and arbitrary detention in violation of *D.K. Basu* guidelines.

nhrc.nic.in ↗
