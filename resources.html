Official Statutory Law & Judicial Databases

India Code (Ministry of Law & Justice)

Official digital repository of all Central & State Bare Acts (BNS, BNSS, Indian Contract Act 1872, CPA 2019, IT Act 2000, DPDP Act 2023).

indiacode.nic.in ↗

Indian Kanoon

Search engine for all Indian Supreme Court, High Court, and Tribunal judgments (*D.K. Basu*, *Lalita Kumari*, *Jacob Mathew*, *Puttaswamy*).

indiankanoon.org ↗

eCourts Services Portal & App

Case status, cause lists, daily orders, and certified copy applications across 3,000+ District and Subordinate Court complexes in India.

ecourts.gov.in ↗

e-Daakhil Consumer Portal

Ministry of Consumer Affairs portal to file online claims before District, State, and National Consumer Commissions without an advocate.

edaakhil.nic.in ↗

NALSA & Free Legal Aid

Statutory authority providing free legal aid advocates to women, children, undertrials, and low-income citizens under Article 39A.

nalsa.gov.in ↗
