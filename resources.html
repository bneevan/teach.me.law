Verified Indian Legal Portals & Databases

Curated high-trust official government portals, Supreme Court databases, cyber helpline channels, and verified community legal desks across India.

Civil & Criminal Foundation: Statutory Law & Judicial Databases

India Code (Ministry of Law & Justice)

Official digital repository of all Central & State Bare Acts (BNS, BNSS, Indian Contract Act 1872, CPA 2019, IT Act 2000, DPDP Act 2023).

indiacode.nic.in ↗

Indian Kanoon

Search engine for all Indian Supreme Court, High Court, and Tribunal judgments (*D.K. Basu*, *Lalita Kumari*, *Jacob Mathew*, *Puttaswamy*).

indiankanoon.org ↗

eCourts Services Portal & App

Case status, cause lists, daily orders, and certified copy applications across 3,000+ District and Subordinate Court complexes in India.

ecourts.gov.in ↗

e-Daakhil Consumer Portal

Ministry of Consumer Affairs portal to file online claims before District, State, and National Consumer Commissions without an advocate.

edaakhil.nic.in ↗

NALSA & Free Legal Aid

Statutory authority providing free legal aid advocates to women, children, undertrials, and low-income citizens under Article 39A.

nalsa.gov.in ↗

Track 1: Cybercrime, Banking & Digital Rights Helplines

National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal & 1930

Ministry of Home Affairs portal for financial fraud reporting, Golden Hour inter-bank freezes, and cyber extortion FIR registration.

cybercrime.gov.in ↗

RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (CMS)

Statutory escalation portal for unreversed unauthorized bank transactions, failed ATM withdrawals, and customer protection circular defaults.

cms.rbi.org.in ↗

DoT Sanchar Saathi & Chakshu Portal

Department of Telecommunications portal to verify SIM connections in your name, block stolen phones (CEIR), and report fraudulent calls.

sancharsaathi.gov.in ↗

CERT-In (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team)

National nodal agency for reporting corporate data leaks, vulnerabilities, and unauthorized access to computer systems.

cert-in.org.in ↗

Track 2: Employment, Labour Rights & POSH Grievance Portals

SAMADHAN Industrial Dispute Portal

Ministry of Labour & Employment online portal for filing conciliation applications regarding unpaid wages, unlawful termination, and terminal dues.

samadhan.labour.gov.in ↗

SHe-Box (Sexual Harassment Electronic Box)

Ministry of Women & Child Development centralized portal for registering workplace sexual harassment complaints directly with statutory monitoring cells.

shebox.wcd.gov.in ↗

Chief Labour Commissioner (Central)

Appellate and enforcement authority for Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, Maternity Benefit Act, and Contract Labour regulations.

clc.gov.in ↗

EPFiGMS (EPFO Grievance Management System)

Online grievance portal for unresolved Employee Provident Fund (EPF) transfers, pension claims, and employer non-deposit of PF contributions.

epfigms.gov.in ↗

Track 3: Public Remedies, Right to Information (RTI) & Free Legal Aid (NALSA)

RTI Online Portal (Government of India)

Centralized gateway to file online Right to Information applications and first appeals under Section 6 & 19 across all Central Ministries and Departments.

rtionline.gov.in ↗

Central Information Commission (CIC)

Apex statutory appellate tribunal for Second Appeals and Section 20 penalty enforcement (₹250/day up to ₹25,000) against defaulting Public Information Officers.

cic.gov.in ↗

National Legal Services Authority (NALSA)

Statutory authority providing 100% free legal aid advocates to women, children, SC/ST, custody undertrials, and indigent citizens under Article 39A and Section 12.

nalsa.gov.in ↗

National Lok Adalat Portal

Schedule, statutory guidelines, and pre-litigation settlement channels before Lok Adalats; awards are final non-appealable decrees with 100% court fee refund.

nalsa.gov.in/lok-adalat ↗

National Human Rights Commission (NHRC)

Statutory body investigating custodial torture, police excesses, and arbitrary detention in violation of *D.K. Basu* guidelines.

nhrc.nic.in ↗
