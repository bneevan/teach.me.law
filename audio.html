Lesson 0001 • Core Foundation

The Indian Citizen's Legal Map: Civil vs. Criminal Law

⏱ Duration: 6m 58s • Spoken Narration
⚡ Quick Revision Takeaways
  • Civil vs. Criminal: Civil law compensates private wrongs (*Jacob Mathew*); criminal law punishes public societal offences requiring *Mens Rea* proven beyond reasonable doubt.
  • Mandatory FIR: Under *Lalita Kumari v. Govt. of U.P.*, police officers are legally mandated to register an FIR upon receiving information disclosing a cognizable offence.
  • Hierarchical Map: Understand the progression from Judicial Magistrate & District Courts to High Courts and the Supreme Court under Article 32 & 226.

⏱ Duration: 6m 10s • Spoken Narration

Encounters with Authority: The D.K. Basu Shield, Traffic Stops & Due Process

⏱ Duration: 6m 02s • Spoken Narration
⚡ Quick Revision Takeaways
  • D.K. Basu Guidelines: Clear identification tags, mandatory Arrest Memo with witness signature, relative notification within 8–12 hours, and medical checkups every 48 hours.
