# Mission: Fundamentals of Indian Law for Everyday Citizens

## Why
Demystify the Indian legal system so that everyday citizens can confidently protect their fundamental constitutional rights, recognize binding contracts and tenancy agreements, enforce consumer protections against corporate negligence, and navigate interactions with police and courts without fear, intimidation, or extortion.

## Success looks like
- Confidently distinguish between civil disputes and criminal charges in India, understanding cognizable vs. non-cognizable offenses, bailable vs. non-bailable warrants, and court hierarchies (Magistrates to High Courts and the Supreme Court).
- Review contracts, job agreements, and 11-month Leave & License rent agreements under the Indian Contract Act 1872 and Information Technology Act 2000, spotting illegal penalty clauses and unfair terms.
- Enforce consumer rights under the Consumer Protection Act 2019 (CPA 2019) via e-Daakhil, drafting formal Legal Notices and claiming compensation for deficiency in service or misleading advertisements (*LDA v. M.K. Gupta*, *IMA v. V.P. Shantha*).
- Exercise constitutional and statutory safeguards during police interactions, searches, traffic stops, and arrest using the Supreme Court's landmark *D.K. Basu* guidelines, *Arnesh Kumar* notice safeguards, and Article 20(3) right to silence (*Nandini Satpathy*).
- Utilize free legal aid through NALSA (National Legal Services Authority), Lok Adalats, the Right to Information (RTI Act 2005), and eCourts services without needing expensive legal representation for basic remedies.

## Constraints
- Beginner level: tailored for everyday Indian citizens, students, tenants, and consumers.
- No arbitrary lesson length constraint: scenarios are explained thoroughly with full landmark case backgrounds and practical walkthroughs.
- Dual-mode audio-first: audio narration generated via `kokoro-tts-tool` is completely coherent eyes-free, while HTML visual cards and tables stand independently ears-free.

## Out of scope
- Specialized bar-exam doctrine, corporate mergers & acquisitions, admiralty law, and complex constitutional bench appellate procedures.
- Individualized legal representation (strictly an educational citizen empowerment foundation).
