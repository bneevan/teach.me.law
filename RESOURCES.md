# Indian Legal Fundamentals Resources

## Knowledge (Official Databases & Primary Law)

- [India Code (Ministry of Law and Justice)](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/)
  The definitive digital repository of all Central and State Bare Acts (Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita / IPC, BNSS / CrPC, Indian Contract Act 1872, Consumer Protection Act 2019, Information Technology Act 2000). Use for: reading authentic statutory provisions and amendment dates.
- [Indian Kanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/)
  Search engine for Indian law covering all Supreme Court and High Court judgments, tribunal rulings, and constitutional bench orders. Use for: reading primary landmark judgments (*D.K. Basu*, *Lalita Kumari*, *Jacob Mathew*, *Arnesh Kumar*).
- [eCourts Services Portal & Mobile App](https://ecourts.gov.in/)
  National court case tracking portal launched by the e-Committee of the Supreme Court of India. Use for: checking case status, daily cause lists, orders, and judgment copies across 3,000+ District and Subordinate Court complexes.
- [e-Daakhil Consumer Grievance Portal](https://edaakhil.nic.in/)
  Official online filing portal established under Consumer Protection Act 2019. Use for: filing consumer complaints before District, State, and National Consumer Commissions without physical presence.
- [RTI Online Portal](https://rtionline.gov.in/)
  Government of India portal for filing Right to Information applications and First Appeals across central ministries and departments for ₹10. Use for: obtaining government records, public expenditure details, and public authority accountability.
- [NALSA (National Legal Services Authority)](https://nalsa.gov.in/)
  Statutory authority providing free and competent legal services to women, children, scheduled castes/tribes, custody undertrials, and individuals with annual income below statutory thresholds under Article 39A of the Constitution. Use for: requesting a free court-appointed legal aid advocate.

## Wisdom (Communities & Practical Guidance)

- [District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) Front Offices](https://nalsa.gov.in/lsas)
  Walk-in free legal advice desks located inside every District Court Complex across India. Use for: document triage, mediation assistance, and free legal aid assignment.
- [LiveLaw & Bar and Bench](https://www.livelaw.in/)
  India's leading verified legal journalism platforms providing real-time reporting on Supreme Court, High Court, and Tribunal orders with expert commentary. Use for: staying informed on recent judicial precedents affecting citizen rights.
- [National Lok Adalats](https://nalsa.gov.in/lok-adalat)
  Alternate dispute resolution forums held quarterly across all court complexes for instant, binding, non-appealable settlement of cheque bounce cases, motor accident claims, traffic challans, and bank recoveries without court fees.
- [r/LegalAdviceIndia (Reddit)](https://reddit.com/r/LegalAdviceIndia)
  Active community moderated by practicing Indian advocates and law students discussing everyday dilemmas like security deposits, FIR registration, traffic challans, and cyber frauds. Use for: peer perspective (note: educational only, not formal legal counsel).

## Gaps & State Nuances
- Rent control laws and stamp duty rates on lease agreements are governed by individual State Acts (e.g. Maharashtra Rent Control Act, Delhi Rent Control Act, Karnataka Rent Control Act). Check local State Registration & Stamps department portals.
