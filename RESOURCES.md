# Indian Legal Fundamentals Resources

## Knowledge (Official Databases & Primary Law)

### General & Statutory Law
- [India Code (Ministry of Law and Justice)](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/)
  The definitive digital repository of Central and State Bare Acts (Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita / IPC, BNSS / CrPC, Indian Contract Act 1872, Consumer Protection Act 2019, Information Technology Act 2000, DPDP Act 2023).
- [Indian Kanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/)
  Search engine for Indian law covering all Supreme Court and High Court judgments, tribunal rulings, and constitutional bench orders (*D.K. Basu*, *Lalita Kumari*, *Jacob Mathew*, *K.S. Puttaswamy*).
- [eCourts Services Portal & Mobile App](https://ecourts.gov.in/)
  National court case tracking portal launched by the e-Committee of the Supreme Court of India. Check case status, orders, and cause lists across 3,000+ District and Subordinate Court complexes.
- [e-Daakhil Consumer Grievance Portal](https://edaakhil.nic.in/)
  Online filing portal established under Consumer Protection Act 2019 for filing complaints before District, State, and National Consumer Commissions without physical presence.
- [NALSA (National Legal Services Authority)](https://nalsa.gov.in/)
  Statutory authority providing free and competent legal services to women, children, undertrials, and low-income citizens under Article 39A of the Constitution.

### Track 1: Cyber Law, Banking & Data Privacy
- [National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (I4C / MHA)](https://cybercrime.gov.in/)
  Government portal to register cyber financial frauds and cyber crimes against women/children. Direct integration with the **1930 National Cyber Helpline** for freezing defrauded bank transfers in real time.
- [RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme & CMS Portal](https://cms.rbi.org.in/)
  Reserve Bank of India dispute portal for resolving bank negligence, unauthorized electronic debits, and credit card disputes under the *July 2017 RBI Master Circular on Zero Liability*.
- [CERT-In (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team)](https://www.cert-in.org.in/)
  National nodal agency for responding to computer security incidents, ransomware advisories, phishing alerts, and vulnerability disclosures.
- [Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 Portal](https://www.meity.gov.in/content/digital-personal-data-protection-act-2023)
  Statutory framework governing personal data processing, Data Fiduciary obligations, and the Data Protection Board of India.
- [TRAI DND (Do Not Disturb) 3.0 & Spam Portal](https://trai.gov.in/consumer-info/telecom/dnd)
  Telecom Regulatory Authority of India portal for reporting commercial telemarketing calls, SMS fraud, and unregistered telemarketers.

## Wisdom (Communities & Practical Guidance)

- [District Cyber Crime Police Cells & Helpdesks](https://cybercrime.gov.in/)
  Specialized cyber police stations located in every police commissionerate and district headquarters in India for filing cyber FIRs and tracking mule bank accounts.
- [Internet Freedom Foundation (IFF)](https://internetfreedom.in/)
  Public interest digital rights organization defending digital privacy, net neutrality, and algorithmic transparency in Indian courts.
- [District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) Front Offices](https://nalsa.gov.in/lsas)
  Walk-in free legal advice desks located inside every District Court Complex across India.
- [National Lok Adalats](https://nalsa.gov.in/lok-adalat)
  Alternate dispute resolution forums held quarterly for instant, binding, non-appealable settlement of disputes without court fees.
- [r/LegalAdviceIndia (Reddit)](https://reddit.com/r/LegalAdviceIndia)
  Active community moderated by practicing Indian advocates and law students discussing everyday dilemmas like security deposits, FIR registration, traffic challans, and cyber frauds.
