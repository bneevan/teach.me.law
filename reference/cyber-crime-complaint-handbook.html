Immediate Cyber Shield

Indian Cyber Fraud & Digital Arrest Defense Field Handbook

Actionable emergency protocols for freezing defrauded funds during the 1930 "Golden Hour", identifying fake "digital arrest" video calls, and asserting your statutory Zero Liability rights under RBI rules.

1. The 1930 Helpline "Golden Hour" Protocol (Freezing Funds)

When money is fraudulently debited from your bank via UPI, NetBanking, or cloned card, the first 2 hours are the "Golden Hour". The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) operates the Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting System (CFCFRS) connecting all Indian banks.

Step 1: Call 1930 Immediately Dial 1930 from your registered mobile number. Keep your Account Number, the fraud debit SMS, the recipient UPI ID / Account Number (beneficiary), and Transaction UTR Number ready.
Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting System (I4C)
Step 2: Instant Inter-Bank Lien Freeze The 1930 system generates an emergency ticket and triggers an automated API ping to the beneficiary's bank (and intermediary payment gateway like Paytm, Razorpay, or PhonePe) to place a lien/freeze on the transferred funds before the scammer can withdraw cash from an ATM.
Automated Inter-Bank Settlement Hold
Step 3: Log In to cybercrime.gov.in within 24 Hours Using the SMS acknowledgment number sent by 1930, log in to cybercrime.gov.in to upload bank statements, transaction screenshots, and audio logs to convert the ticket into an official Cyber Police FIR.
Section 66D IT Act / Section 318(4) BNS

2. "Digital Arrest" Scams: The Legal Defense Protocol

Fraudsters call claiming that a FedEx parcel with narcotics or a fake bank account in your name has been seized, connecting you to an impersonated "CBI / Mumbai Police / ED Officer" in a fake police station over Skype video who forbids you from disconnecting under threat of immediate arrest.

The Law: "Digital Arrest" Does NOT Exist in Indian Law Neither the Code of Criminal Procedure, the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, nor police rules recognize any concept of "Digital Arrest" or video call custody. Police cannot arrest, interrogate, or demand asset disclosures over Skype or WhatsApp video calls.
Statutory Guarantee: Arrest Requires Physical Custody
The Golden Test: Demand Written Summons Under Section 41A Tell the caller: "Send me a physical Notice of Appearance under Section 41A CrPC (or Sec 35 BNSS) through my local jurisdictional police station. I will appear in person with my advocate." Real law enforcement officers must follow the Arnesh Kumar protocol.
Section 41A CrPC / Sec 35 BNSS
The Money Tell: Government Never Demands "Verification Transfers" No court, CBI officer, or police department will ever ask you to transfer funds to a "secret government verification account" or "RBI safe custody account." Any request to transfer money is 100% a criminal fraud under Section 66D of the IT Act.
Section 66D IT Act & Section 319 BNS

3. RBI Zero Liability 3-Day Rule (Circular DBR.No.Leg.BC.78/2017)

Under the Reserve Bank of India Master Circular on Customer Protection, a bank customer's liability for unauthorized electronic banking transactions is legally capped or completely waived depending on reporting speed:

Reporting Timeframe Customer Liability Bank Mandate
Within 3 Working Days ZERO Liability (₹0) Bank must credit full amount (Shadow Reversal) within 10 working days, regardless of scammer recovery.
Between 4 to 7 Working Days Limited to ₹5,000 (Basic Savings) or ₹10,000 (Savings/Credit Card <₹5L) Customer bears capped liability; bank is legally liable for refunding the rest.
Beyond 7 Working Days As per individual Bank Board approved policy Dispute resolution must occur within 90 days maximum; escalate to RBI Ombudsman if unresolved.
